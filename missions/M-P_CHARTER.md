# CANDIDATE MISSION CHARTER — M-P: Professional Compliance Information Products

**Status: CANDIDATE — NOT CHARTERED.**
**Created:** 2026-08-15 · **Written from scratch**, inheriting nothing from M-A.

---

## 1. Economic model

> AI performs **primary-source research on fast-changing rules** → produces a dated, sourced reference or working tool → sold to **professionals whose exposure to those rules is financial or legal** → earning per unit, through whichever channel reaches them.

**The bet, stated in one sentence:** our verified edge is not writing — it is *assembling accurate, dated, primary-sourced answers about rules that changed recently*, faster than the free web corrects itself.

## 2. Why this family at all — the evidence for the edge

The claim must rest on something demonstrated, not hoped for. During M-A it was demonstrated:

| Demonstration | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Three documented corrections** to claims repeated confidently across the free web | YouTube disclosure does *not* cost monetisation (it does not limit audience or earnings) · the 2026 "demonetisation" was a **rename** of an existing policy · Etsy bans **selling** prompt bundles, not *using* prompts |
| **A dated statutory obligation** most affected professionals have not read | CA AB 723 → Bus. & Prof. Code §10140.8, effective 2026-01-01: disclosure of digitally altered listing images **plus** a link/URL/QR to the original, reasonably conspicuous, on or adjacent to the image **[P]** |
| **A widely-mistaken regulatory timeline** corrected | EU AI Act Annex III high-risk obligations moved to **2 December 2027**, not August 2026 **[R]** |
| **An evidence-grading discipline** that survives scrutiny | 73 `[Official]` / 17 `[Reported]` / 21 `[Unknown]` / 6 `[Interpretation]` across ~9,000 words |

**This is a capability, not an artifact.** The distinction matters (R10): if the capability claim is wrong, this mission should lose, regardless of what we already have on disk.

## 3. Scope boundary

**In scope:** written reference products and working tools about **specific, dated, verifiable rules** — statutes, platform policies, regulatory deadlines — sold to a **defined professional audience** with financial or legal exposure.

**Out of scope:**
- General-audience explainers competing with free blog content *(this is exactly what killed A0001)*.
- Legal advice, or anything requiring a professional licence to provide.
- Rules that are stable — the edge is *recency*, and stable rules have adequate free coverage.
- Non-written products (software, templates).
- Anything where we cannot verify claims against a primary source.

## 4. What we already know that is discouraging

Stated up front so this is not chartered on optimism:

| Concern | Status |
|---|---|
| **Purchase intent for compliance references is unproven for us.** Professionals *have* budgets; that they will buy *this* is untested | `[U]` |
| **Regulatory-adjacent content carries real duty of care.** A legal-interpretation error damages the only thing the product sells | Mitigated by R5 three-tier separation — but a live risk |
| **Fast-decaying assets** need re-issue discipline, or they become wrong rather than merely stale | Manageable; also the source of the edge |
| **A0001 failed** on two of four paid-beats-free grounds (artifact, budget context) | Instructive, not disqualifying — those were product-form failures, and M-P's form is different |
| **Channel is unresolved.** Marketplace (H1-blocked) vs direct (no traffic) is a genuine open question | Must be settled before, not during |
| **Narrow audiences are narrow.** A jurisdiction-specific product has a hard ceiling | Real constraint on scale |

## 5. The central question

> Will professionals with financial or legal exposure **pay** for a dated, primary-sourced compliance reference that the free web covers inaccurately?

Not *do they need it* — M-A already taught that need is not intent (R4).

## 6. Success criteria — from scratch

| Level | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **P-0 — Reachability** | Can we put the product in front of the defined audience **at all**, at $0? Answers the channel question before any writing |
| **P-1 — Validation** | ≥1 sale to a stranger in the target profession |
| **P-2 — Realisation** | Cash received |
| **P-3 — Viability** | **≥$300 net within 90 days of listing** from a single product |

**P-3 is set higher than M-C's $100** because unit prices here are higher ($19.99–$49 versus $6.99) and the audience is narrower. If a professional product with a verified statutory hook cannot clear $300, the edge claim is false and the family should be abandoned rather than iterated.

## 7. Kill criteria — pre-committed

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| **P-0 fails** — no zero-cost route to the audience | **KILL before writing.** This is the cheapest possible kill and the most likely failure |
| Any primary-source claim cannot be verified to `[Official]` | **KILL the topic.** The whole product is sourcing rigour |
| P-1 not met within 60 days of listing | KILL. Narrow audience plus no sale is decisive |
| P-1 met, P-3 missed by >70% | HOLD, do not scale |
| Owner declines regulatory-adjacent content at editorial gate | KILL, no argument |
| Spend would exceed **$50** before P-1 | Stop, escalate |

## 8. Estimated cost to test

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Cash | **$0** (marketplace) or $0 (direct rail free tier) |
| Owner attention | ~2–3 hours: payout setup, one editorial approval, one publish |
| Agent production | One narrow reference/tool — smaller than a novel, larger than an article |
| **P-0 answerable** | **Before any production**, at near-zero cost |

**The attractive feature: the most likely failure mode is also the cheapest to test.** P-0 (can we reach these people at all?) can be answered before a word is written.

## 9. Open questions

1. **Which profession?** AB 723 gives a verified hook for California real estate licensees, but that was selected under a drifted scope and must be re-justified here.
2. **Which channel?** Marketplace (H1-blocked, supplies traffic) or direct (no traffic, needs a route to the audience). **P-0 is exactly this question.**
3. **Is the audience reachable without paid acquisition?** The single biggest risk.
4. **Does the narrow ceiling matter** if this is a repeatable *line* rather than one product?
