# CAPABILITY UNLOCK — the one input that reopens the opportunity space

**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Status:** the single highest-leverage action available to this project. **~20–30 minutes of Owner time.**

---

## Why this document exists

Three missions have concluded. Two were **market-bound** — the opportunity genuinely is not there. One, M-C, was **capability-bound**: the opportunity *is* there and we cannot reach it from this environment.

Capability-bound kills come with an obligation (R13): **specify the cheapest capability that would change the answer.** That is what this is.

## The gap, precisely

| What M-C needs | Status |
|---|---|
| Organic discovery is achievable in **verifiably low-competition** niches | ✅ Evidenced **[R]** |
| Verification means reading **review counts** and **publish dates** off the first page of Amazon results for a candidate search term | ✅ Evidenced **[R]** |
| Getting that data from this session | ❌ **Verified impossible.** Amazon domains return 000 under the egress allowlist; scraping is barred by our own compliance policy regardless |

Without it, niche selection is a guess against a base rate where ~80% of small-catalogue authors earn under $100/month. With it, it becomes a screened bet.

**This is not an ongoing attention drain.** It is a **one-off, per-candidate-niche** lookup, and it can be done by anyone with a browser.

---

## What I need — the exact procedure

For each candidate search term I supply, open Amazon, search the term in **Kindle Store**, and record the **first ten results**.

**For each of the ten:**

| Field | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| Title | Result listing |
| **Number of ratings/reviews** | The number beside the stars, e.g. "1,247 ratings" |
| **Publication date** | Product page → "Product details" → Publication date |
| Price | Result listing |
| Kindle Unlimited? | "Available instantly" / KU badge |

Then, from any one product page: **Best Sellers Rank** (Product details → e.g. "#12,431 in Kindle Store").

### Paste it back in any format — a rough list is fine

```
TERM: cozy mystery knitting
1. Knit One Kill Two — 412 ratings — Mar 2021 — $4.99 — KU
2. A Stitch in Crime — 88 ratings — Jul 2024 — $3.99 — KU
...
BSR of #1 result: #8,432 in Kindle Store
```

No tools, no extensions, no account needed. Copy-paste or a screenshot both work.

## The decision rule I will apply

This is stated in advance so the procedure is not a fishing expedition — the data either passes or fails a pre-committed test:

| Signal | Reading |
|---|---|
| First-page average **under ~100 ratings** | **Winnable** — the niche has demand but weak incumbents |
| Most recent first-page publish date **2+ years old** | **Strong signal** — demand with no active publisher |
| First page dominated by **1,000+ rating titles** | **Not winnable.** Do not enter |
| Term returns generic results but not the specific variant | **Sub-niche gap** — often the best entry point |

**If no candidate term passes, M-C stays dead and I will say so.** The procedure is designed to be capable of returning a "no."
