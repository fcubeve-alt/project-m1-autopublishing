## 1. Economic model

> AI produces written works → sold as **discrete units** through **retail publishing marketplaces** → earning **royalties per copy sold**.

**How this differs from M-A (Class A), materially and not just semantically:**

| | Class A (concluded) | Class C (this candidate) |
|---|---|---|
| Who decides value | The platform's algorithm and reader-time metrics | **A buyer, making a purchase decision** |
| Who bears marginal cost | The platform | **Nobody — the buyer pays per copy** |
| Consequence for AI policy | Barred or de-monetised | **Permitted with disclosure** |
| Revenue per unit | Fractions of a cent per read | **Dollars per sale** |
| What must be true to earn | Volume of attention | **Discoverability + purchase decision** |

The second row is why this family is worth chartering at all: it is the structural reason Class C stayed open while Class A closed.

## 2. Scope boundary

**In scope:** Amazon KDP · Kobo Writing Life · Apple Books · Google Play Books · Barnes & Noble Press · Draft2Digital (aggregator) · Leanpub · Everand.
**Content:** written works — fiction, narrative non-fiction, reference, collections.

**Out of scope, explicitly:**
- Direct-sale storefronts (Gumroad, Payhip, Ko-fi) — payout rails, not marketplaces; they supply no demand.
- Non-written products — templates, tools, software.
- **Professional compliance tooling sold as a book.** This is the A0002 drift, named here so the boundary cannot erode the same way twice.
- Audience-acquisition platforms (Class B) — may support this mission, never count as its revenue.

## 4. The central question

> Can a publisher with **no audience, no catalogue and no ad budget** achieve organic marketplace discovery for disclosed AI-written work, at a rate that clears a defined economic hurdle?

Everything else is secondary. Production capability is not in doubt; **discovery** is the entire question.

## 5. Success criteria — defined from scratch

| Level | Criterion | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **C-0 — Discovery** | Title achieves **non-trivial organic impressions** within 30 days of listing | The prerequisite. Zero impressions falsifies the family for us regardless of quality |
| **C-1 — Validation** | ≥1 attributable royalty sale not traceable to us or anyone we know | Loop closes |
| **C-2 — Realisation** | Cash received in the Owner's account | Mission milestone (~60–90 days after C-1) |
| **C-3 — Viability** | **≥$100 net royalty from a single title within 90 days of listing** | The hurdle that distinguishes a business from a hobby |

**C-3 is the criterion that matters, and it is deliberately set where the evidence says most titles fail.** ~$100/month is roughly the line ~80% of small-catalogue authors do not cross. A mission that cannot beat the median outcome should not receive further capital, and setting a hurdle we could clear by accident would make the whole exercise decorative.

## 6. Kill criteria — pre-committed

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| ~0 impressions after **two** distinct metadata/category configurations over 30 days | **KILL.** Organic discovery is unreachable cold; no amount of writing fixes it |
| Impressions but ~0 clicks after packaging revision | KILL or pivot to packaging as the object of study |
| C-1 met but C-3 missed by >70% at 90 days | **HOLD**, do not scale. Re-run the counterfactual test |
| Any platform reverses its AI-disclosure position | Immediate re-screen |
| Total spend would exceed **$50** before C-1 | Stop and escalate to Owner |

**Pre-committing kill criteria is the point.** They are set now, before any sunk cost exists to rationalise around.

## 8. Known open questions

1. **H1** — payout geography. Blocks the entire family, not just this mission.
2. **Genre and length.** Stage 4 already caught one contradiction: a 65–100pp Short Read conflicts with cozy mystery's 60–80k-word reader expectation. Any genre choice must be internally consistent on format, length and reader promise.
3. **The single-title problem.** Catalogue size predicts income, but a first experiment has a catalogue of one. Either the hurdle is cleared by a single title, or the family requires a scale commitment that must be justified *before* starting, not discovered halfway.
4. **Amazon marketplace data is unobtainable** from this environment, and scraping is barred by our own compliance policy. Niche selection cannot rest on BSR or keyword volume.
