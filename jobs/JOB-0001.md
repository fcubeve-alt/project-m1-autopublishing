## Audience hypothesis

Independent creators, marketplace sellers and small-business operators who use generative AI in commercial work and have just discovered — or are about to discover — that the rules governing disclosure changed underneath them in the last month.

Three concrete reader situations:
1. An Etsy seller whose listings began being filtered from search after 2026-08-11.
2. An EU-based small business that learned on 2026-08-02 that Article 50 is now binding, with penalties to €15M or 3% of global turnover.
3. A self-publisher who does not know whether their workflow counts as AI-*generated* or AI-*assisted* on KDP — a distinction that determines whether non-disclosure is a violation.

All three are **deciding, not browsing.** They have money or legal exposure at stake and want a correct answer quickly.

## Intended reader outcome

The reader can, within 30 minutes: determine which rules apply to them across every platform they use, know exactly what to disclose and where, and act — with each obligation traceable to a dated primary source they can verify themselves.

## Editorial quality requirements

- Every factual claim carries an **effective date** and a **source**.
- Source type disclosed: official page vs trade press.
- `UNKNOWN` written as `UNKNOWN`. Never filled from model memory.
- Reference-book register: precise, scannable, tables over prose where a table is faster.
- Written for a non-lawyer operator making a decision today.

## Prohibited patterns / known AI-writing tells

- No padding, no restating the same point in three registers, no throat-clearing before content.
- No "In today's fast-paced digital landscape" openings.
- No em-dash-heavy breathless cadence; no tricolon addiction.
- No fabricated statistics, case studies, testimonials or sources.
- No legal advice framing.
- No urgency or fear language beyond what the cited penalties factually state.
- No undated claims. This is the single most important rule — it is the entire differentiator against existing SEO content.

## Compliance constraints

- **AI disclosure:** declare AI-generated text at KDP upload. In-book AI disclosure note regardless of whether the EU Art. 50 editorial-review carve-out applies (`COMPLIANCE_POLICY.md` §6).
- **Exclusivity:** none. No KDP Select.
- **Originality:** official policy language quoted briefly with attribution for accurate reporting. Cover art must be an original typographic design — no purchased or templated design, no photorealistic AI people.
- **Non-affiliation:** front matter states no affiliation with or endorsement by any platform named.
