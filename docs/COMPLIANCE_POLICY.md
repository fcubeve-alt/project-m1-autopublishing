# COMPLIANCE POLICY

**Date:** 2026-08-15 · **Status:** Binding on all assets produced by this business
**Note:** this business publishes *about* AI disclosure compliance. Any failure to comply ourselves would be both a policy breach and a fatal credibility failure. This policy is enforced more strictly than the platforms require.

---

## 1. Non-negotiable prohibitions

Carried forward from the brief §7 and treated as absolute:

- **No fake identity, false address, or borrowed financial accounts.**
- **No CAPTCHA bypass or automated circumvention of human-verification controls.**
- **No deceptive AI disclosure** — no omitting, minimising or mislabelling AI involvement.
- **No policy evasion** — no working around a platform rule we find inconvenient. If a platform forbids what we are, we do not publish there.
- **No scraping of prohibited or private data**, and no automation of account operations where the platform disallows it.
- **No fabrication** — no invented statistics, case studies, sources, testimonials or reviews.

**Practical consequence already applied:** Medium and the paying literary magazines both permit content we could produce, under conditions we could technically satisfy by simply not disclosing. We have excluded both platforms instead. That exclusion is the policy working.

## 2. AI disclosure standard — per platform

| Platform | Requirement | Our practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Amazon KDP** | Disclose AI-*generated* text/images/translations at upload. AI-*assisted* (human-created, AI-refined) need not be disclosed. Undisclosed AI is a violation even where the content would be permitted | **Disclose as AI-generated.** Our drafting process is AI-generated with human editorial review — we do not rely on the "assisted" carve-out, and where the boundary is arguable we disclose |
| **Amazon (images)** | Photorealistic AI-generated people require IPTC keyword `contains-synthetic-performer` in `dc:subject` (XMP) | **Avoided entirely** — no photorealistic people in cover or interior art |
| **Etsy** | Per-listing disclosure mandatory; missing field filters listing from search. From 2026-08-11 templated designs from computerised tools disallowed unless the seller's original design; purchased/templated prompts violate policy | Not currently publishing to Etsy. If we do: disclose per listing, use only original prompts and designs |
| **EU AI Act Art. 50** (binding 2026-08-02) | Synthetic text must be machine-readably marked and detectable. Disclosure required for AI text on public-interest matters **unless subject to substantive human editorial review with a person assuming editorial responsibility** | **In-book AI disclosure note** on every asset. The Human Editorial Gate is the mechanism by which a named person assumes editorial responsibility — this is a legal function, not a formality |
| **Royal Road** | Tag AI-assisted or AI-generated; chapter pages only; human voice required in comments, reviews, forum posts and PMs | If used: tag accurately, and **all community interaction is human-written by the Owner** |
| **Medium** | AI-generated cannot be paywalled, disclosed or not | **Excluded from the platform set** |
| **Literary magazines** | Refuse AI and AI-assisted work | **Excluded from the platform set** |

**House rule:** where two platforms disagree, we apply the stricter. Where a rule is ambiguous, we disclose.

## 3. Originality and copyright

- All output must be original work. No reproduction of copyrighted text beyond brief, attributed quotation of official policy language, used for the purpose of accurate reporting.
- **Official rules are quoted with attribution and date.** Regulatory and policy text is quoted precisely rather than paraphrased where precision matters legally — and the source and effective date always accompany it.
- No brand names, logos, characters or trade dress in cover art. No "in the style of" prompting against a named living artist.
- Platform names are used nominatively — to identify the platform being described — with no implication of endorsement, affiliation or official status. **A prominent non-affiliation statement appears in the front matter.**

---

## 4. Accuracy standard for regulatory content

Because assets concern legal and platform-compliance obligations, a raised standard applies:

1. **Every factual claim carries an effective date.** Undated regulatory claims are prohibited.
2. **Every rule cites its source**, with source type disclosed (official page vs press reporting).
3. **`UNKNOWN` is written as `UNKNOWN`.** Never filled from model memory. This is the brief's hard rule and it applies to the product as strictly as to the research.
4. **Not legal advice.** A prominent notice appears in the front matter, stating the material is a compliance reference, that rules change, and that jurisdiction-specific questions require a qualified professional.
5. **Recency is disclosed as a limitation.** The edition date appears on the cover, and the text states plainly that rules in this area change quickly and readers must verify against the primary source before acting.
6. **Secondary sources are labelled as such.** Where a fact rests on trade press rather than an official page, the text says so.

---

## 5. Cross-posting and exclusivity

- Default: **non-exclusive.** No KDP Select enrollment for A0001.
- Before any republication: confirm the source platform permits it and the destination permits previously-published work.
- Per brief §8, never assume a work may be cross-posted everywhere. Rights are checked per platform, per asset, and recorded in the asset's `METADATA.md`.

---

## 6. Human editorial responsibility

Under EU AI Act Art. 50, the disclosure obligation for AI-generated text on public-interest matters is relieved where the content has undergone **substantive human editorial review with a person assuming editorial responsibility.**

This makes the brief's Human Editorial Gate load-bearing in law, not just in process. Accordingly:

- No substantive content is published without Owner approval (`APPROVE` / `REVISE` / `REJECT`).
- The approving person is recorded in `ledger/DECISION_LOG.md` with a date.
- **We disclose AI generation anyway**, independent of whether the carve-out applies. Relying on a legal exemption to avoid disclosure would violate §1 above and contradict the product's own thesis.

---

## 7. Escalation triggers

Work stops and the Owner is consulted if:
- A platform's terms appear to prohibit what we are about to do;
- A required step would need identity, payment authority, CAPTCHA or contractual acceptance;
- A claim cannot be verified against an acceptable source and is material to the reader's decision;
- A rule changes mid-production in a way that invalidates published material — triggering a correction and re-issue, not silent editing.
