## Stage 2 — Screening: the decisive axis

Screening every candidate on AI policy produced a pattern sharper than any individual result. **Sorting by AI policy rather than by size or fame is what makes the landscape legible.**

### Tier 1 — VERIFIED CLOSED to substantially-AI-generated work (7)

| Platform | Verified position | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **Medium** | AI-generated writing cannot be paywalled, disclosed or not; enrollment revocable | **[P]** |
| **Reedsy Prompts / Literary Prize** | *"The use of generative AI or other assistive technologies to create, draft, or revise stories is not permitted."* Entries must be written by the submitting user. Disqualification without warning | **[P]** |
| **Publish0x** | *"AI generated content may be removed"* | **[P]** |
| **Wattpad** | Does **not monetise** AI content — no AI text, covers or illustrations on monetised stories | **[P]** |
| **beehiiv** | Publications relying **entirely on AI-generated material without meaningful human input are not permitted**; mass-produced templated AI output explicitly prohibited | **[P]** |
| **Paying literary magazines** | Refuse AI **and AI-assisted** work; documented author bans | **[P]** |
| **HubPages / Quora Partner / Kindle Vella** | Dead or closed to new entrants | **[R]/[P]** |

### Tier 2 — VERIFIED OPEN, but with a tag that carries a commercial or economic penalty (3)

| Platform | Position | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Vocal Media** | AI permitted; **any** AI use must be tagged at submission | Read earnings are **Vocal+ only ($9.99/mo)**; ~16,700 reads/mo to net $100; **hard block without Stripe** **[P]** |
| **Royal Road** | Three tiers; AI-assisted and AI-generated permitted **with tag** | Documented sustained reader backlash against tagged work **[P]** |
| **Tapas** | AI content allowed with **mandatory tag from March 2026**; disclosure required for fully AI-generated series | **1,000 subscribers** required before premium contract eligibility **[R]** |

### Tier 3 — OPEN with disclosure, and economics that do not depend on tagging (9)

**Amazon KDP and the wide-retail channel.** AI-generated text/images/translations must be disclosed at upload; AI-assisted need not be. Disclosure does **not** reduce distribution or royalty. 70% band $2.99–$12.99 since 2026-07-07. **[P]**

Reached via KDP directly or Draft2Digital → Kobo (70% at $2.99+), Apple Books (70%), Google Play (52%), B&N Press, Everand. **[R]**

**This is the only tier in the entire landscape where disclosure is a form to complete rather than a penalty to absorb.**

### Tier 4 — UNKNOWN, and not resolvable to a safe conclusion (9)

| Platform | Status |
|---|---|
| **Substack** | No explicit AI-content prohibition located in published content guidelines. **Permissive by omission ≠ verified permissive** — recorded `[U]` |
| **GoodNovel / Dreame / MoboReader / AlphaNovel / Radish / Neovel** | AI policy `[U]`. **Independent red flag:** Writer Beware issued a "Bad Contract Alert" on Stary/Dreame; reported terms include ghost-writing clauses, no author-initiated exit, first right of refusal on prequels/sequels, and GoodNovel reportedly claiming rights to **all future work** **[R]** |
| **ScribbleHub / Sweek / Laterpress / Mirror / Hive / Paragraph / Ream** | AI policy `[U]`; scale or economics unverified |
| **Per-article publications** (Contently, Science, CXL, Ahrefs, Writer's Digest) | Pay $500–$2,000/piece **[R]**, but require demonstrated expertise, human pitching and editorial acceptance; AI policy `[U]` |

## The structural finding

Screened across 38 candidates, the space is not neutral toward what we are:

> **Almost every platform that supplies a built-in audience has, by 2026, either barred substantially-AI-generated work from monetisation or attached a visible tag to it. The tag is not a formality — on Vocal it sits behind a paid subscription, on Royal Road it triggers reader backlash, on Tapas it arrives with a 1,000-subscriber gate.**
>
> **The single systematic exception is retail royalties, where the buyer's purchase decision is made on the product rather than on a badge, and disclosure is an upload field with no distribution penalty.**

This is not a preference for Amazon. It is the same conclusion arriving from a much wider and properly-scoped search — and it now rests on 38 screened candidates rather than the premature commitment made earlier.

**The honest caveat:** this finding says retail royalties is the *least obstructed* path. It does **not** say it is a *good* path — 75% of self-published authors earn under $1,000/year, and 78% cite discoverability as their main problem. Least obstructed and profitable are different claims, and Stage 4 must not conflate them.

## Stage 3 — Shortlist

Ranked on: AI policy viability · built-in distribution · payout accessibility with country `UNKNOWN` · time to first revenue · economics · contract risk.

| # | Candidate | Model | Why shortlisted | Key risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Amazon KDP (wide, non-exclusive)** | Royalties | Only tier-3 position: AI permitted with disclosure, no distribution penalty, free purchase-intent traffic, $0 entry, works where Stripe does not | Discoverability; unproven demand for any specific title |
| **2** | **Royal Road → reader funnel** | Serial fiction, indirect | AI expressly permitted under a clear published policy; genuine built-in fiction audience; $0 entry; no KYC to publish | Tag carries reader backlash; 60–180 days; monetises off-platform |
| **3** | **Draft2Digital wide retail** | Royalties | One integration → Kobo/Apple/B&N/Everand; pure incremental on a sunk asset | $20 account + $12/yr under $100; deferred until an asset proves itself |
| **4** | **Tapas** | Serial fiction | AI allowed with tag from March 2026; strongest reported revenue share in its class | 1,000-subscriber gate before premium; policy `[R]` not `[P]` |
| **5** | **Substack** | Subscription | Best retained margin (~85–87%), ~48h payouts, no AI prohibition found | Zero cold-start distribution; AI position `[U]` |
| **6** | **Vocal Media** | Per-read | The seed example; verified open with tagging | Economics fail at our scale; Stripe hard block; **retained for completeness, not recommended** |
| **7** | **Ream Stories** | Subscription serial | 90% of subscription revenue to authors **[R]** | Requires a fanbase; AI policy `[U]` |

**Excluded from the shortlist with reasons:** Medium, Reedsy contests, Publish0x, Wattpad, beehiiv, literary magazines — **verified closed**. GoodNovel/Dreame/MoboReader/AlphaNovel — **contract risk independent of AI policy**; a contract reportedly claiming rights to all future work is a bad deal even if AI were permitted. Per-article publications — human pitching cost against a 2–3h/month attention budget.

## What Stage 4 must answer before anything is written

Deliberately **not** answered yet, and no content will be produced until they are:

1. **On KDP:** which *written-content* category — genre fiction, narrative non-fiction, short-form collections? Note that KDP is in scope as a *publishing platform earning royalties on written work*; the professional compliance workbook (A0002) drifted outside the boundary and is paused.
2. **On Royal Road:** what actually performs (LitRPG, progression fantasy, cultivation), chapter cadence, length, and how tagged works actually perform versus untagged.
3. **Cross-platform:** which single content asset could lawfully serve more than one shortlisted platform.
4. **Saturation:** where AI-driven supply has already collapsed the returns.
