## The constraint set that filters everything

Any candidate must survive these before its upside matters:

| Constraint | Value | What it eliminates |
|---|---|---|
| Distribution | **No audience, no ad budget, cannot buy traffic** | Everything requiring me to bring buyers |
| Capital | **~$0**, spend authority $10 | Anything with fixed monthly cost before revenue |
| Owner attention | **~2–3 hours per 30 days** | Anything needing ongoing human client contact |
| Autonomy | AI does research/production; human does KYC, approval, upload | Anything where the human *is* the product |
| Environment | Egress allowlist; no marketplace data; no account operation | Anything needing live market data or bot operation |
| Country | **`UNKNOWN`** | Anything hard-blocked without Stripe |

**The dominant constraint is distribution.** We cannot buy attention. Therefore only candidates where **a marketplace supplies buyer traffic to an unknown seller** can produce revenue in this cycle. That single filter does more work than every other criterion combined, and it is why the portfolio is shaped the way it is.

## Screening notes on the newly explored families

### P5 — Freelance services: killed on attention, not on merit

The most important negative finding of this round, because superficially it looks like the best option.

**In its favour:** genuinely proven buyer intent — people demonstrably pay. Entry-level median ~$35/hr. **Fiverr does not require proactive AI disclosure** in the gig description; disclosure is owed only if a client asks or requests no-AI **[Official]**. Upwork permits AI-assisted proposals while prohibiting fully automated submission, and does not scan deliverables **[Reported]**.

**Why it fails anyway:** new-seller income is **near zero for the first 4–8 weeks** while ranking establishes, and the first 90 days are "about proof, not profit" **[Reported]**. Practitioner reporting is blunt that a first client typically comes from your own network rather than platform search.

Decisively: this candidate consumes **ongoing human attention** — proposals, client messages, revisions, disputes — against a budget of 2–3 hours per month. It also inverts the Mission's autonomy dimension: the human becomes the product and the AI becomes the assistant. That is a job with AI help, not an autonomously operated business.

**Reversal condition:** if the Owner's available attention rises to ~5+ hours/week, re-screen immediately. On buyer intent alone it would rank first.

### P4 — Templates: right product shape, wrong distribution

Genuinely instructive. Gumroad hosts ~146,000 products with templates a major segment (~$8.8M in the Design category); top sellers reach ~$3,200/month; on a $19 template the creator keeps ~$16.13 — **~85% margin versus KDP's 70%** **[Reported]**.

Two findings here are worth more than the candidate itself, and both transfer directly to P1:

1. **Functional templates consistently outperform aesthetic ones**, including in visually-driven niches.
2. The strongest 2026 niches are **profession-specific workflow systems** (therapists, photographers, real-estate agents) and life-administration tools — while generic "life OS" and "second brain" products are the most competitive graveyard.

**Why it does not win:** Notion Marketplace "sends buyers to creators with zero followers" but is characterised as **"supplementary, not primary"** for discovery, behind an approval queue and a 10% + $0.40 cut **[Reported]**. Gumroad standalone supplies **zero** traffic. And I cannot build or test Notion assets from this environment without spending the Owner's scarce hours.

### P7 — Micro-SaaS: killed on time-to-revenue

6–12 months to first paying customer is typical; the field's own consensus is that **building is solved and distribution is the bottleneck** **[Reported]**. Same wall, longer runway.

## The counterfactual test, applied to A0001

> *If I had invested nothing in A0001 yet, knowing everything I know today, would it be the best use of the next unit of time, compute, money and human attention?*

**No.** Stated plainly, because sunk work is not a reason:

- It **fails the artifact test** — an explanation, when the evidence says buyers pay premium for *tools*. "Workbooks earn premium pricing because buyers see them as tools, not just reading material" **[Reported]**.
- It **fails the budget-context test** — $6.99 is consumer impulse pricing. The observed money is at **$19.99 paperback** for nonfiction, and the general finding across families is that the opportunity sits "in specific professional niches where buyers expense purchases or see direct ROI" **[Reported]**.
- Its audience is **general creators**, the most price-sensitive and most free-content-served segment available.
- Its purchase intent remains **unproven and unprovable from this environment**.

**Verdict: A0001 → HOLD.** It is no longer the plan of record and will not consume the Owner's single editorial review while a better-shaped candidate exists.

**What survives the test, separately (R7 — score channel and product independently):**

- **The KDP channel decision stands.** It remains the only verified surface giving an unknown seller free purchase-intent distribution with AI content expressly permitted. Nothing found this round beats it under the distribution constraint.
- **The research asset survives.** The verified rule-set (7 platforms + EU AI Act, 73 `[Official]` claims, 3 documented corrections) is input to P1 at **zero marginal cost**. That is transferable information value, not sunk cost.
- **H1 is product-independent.** The tax/payout gate is required for *any* KDP route, so it stays on the critical path regardless of which product wins. Nothing about this re-evaluation should pause it.

## Stopping rule for this round

Exploration has a cost and I am declaring the boundary rather than drifting.

**Search breadth chosen:** four families beyond the incumbent (services, templates, micro-SaaS, subscription), selected because each represents a *structurally different* answer to the distribution constraint — the variable that dominates. Breadth was set by that structural criterion, not by a quota.

**Stopping now, because:** the last two searches returned no candidate that survives the distribution + attention constraints better than marketplace retail, and they converged on the *same* underlying finding from three independent directions — professional niche, functional artifact, buyer expenses it. When independent searches start agreeing, marginal search value has collapsed.

**Therefore:** expected value of further family-level search is now **below** the expected value of one bounded niche-selection step followed by a $0 experiment. Remaining search effort is redirected from *which family* to *which profession* — a narrower and more decision-relevant question.

**Next re-evaluation triggers:** P1 niche selection completed · H1 answered (country may hard-block options) · any experiment returning real data · a new candidate plausibly beating P1.
