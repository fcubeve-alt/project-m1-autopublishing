## Cross-cutting conclusions

1. **AI-content policy, not audience size, is the primary selection axis.** The stance range
   is wide: Webnovel permissive → Inkitt disclosure-labelled → Medium monetization-barred →
   Wattpad/Tapas restrictive. An AI-native business ranked by traffic would pick exactly the
   wrong platforms.
2. **Vocal's AI policy is the single most valuable unknown.** It is the spec's named template,
   has the clearest published economics, and a targeted search returned nothing
   platform-specific. Record as `UNKNOWN` (R2); resolve first once HG-01 clears.
3. **Payout rails gate everything.** Vocal → Stripe; Substack → Stripe with country
   exceptions. Until HG-02 is answered, ranking cannot be economically honest.
4. **Contests are the most plausible Phase-1 first-revenue path** — no audience, no payout
   threshold, real cash, short cycle — *if and only if* their AI and originality rules permit
   the intended workflow. Verifying that is the highest-value next research task after HG-01.
5. **Evidence quality is currently poor and must not be laundered.** Much of the pool is T3/T4
   from pages that are themselves plausibly AI-generated. One result asserted a "Wattpad
   Trust & Safety internal memo, March 2024" — an internal memo is not a public source; that
   claim is **rejected as unverifiable** and must not be repeated.
